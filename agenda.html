16:05–16:25 · Keynote Talk

Jiazhen Pan

Research Fellow, Stanford University & Technical University of Munich

Jiazhen Pan works on AI agents in medicine, healthcare benchmarks, auditable AI, and medical imaging. His research examines how medical AI systems can move from benchmark performance toward reliable, clinically grounded use.

Keynote title The Last Mile to the Clinic: How Far Are AI Agents from Real Medicine?

The talk will examine the gap between rapid progress in medical AI agents and dependable clinical deployment, with emphasis on rigorous evaluation, auditing, and workflow-level reliability.
