Announcement

Why this track has been added

Following the many inquiries we have received about publishing individual team methods, we are pleased to confirm this new optional archival paper track.

We sincerely apologize for the short notice. We are sharing this opportunity as soon as the Springer proceedings and OpenReview arrangements have been formally confirmed.

Participation is entirely optional and will not affect challenge evaluation, final rankings, or award eligibility.

Eligibility and scope

Who can submit

01

All registered teams

All teams registered for MedReason may submit—not only the Top 3 teams.

02

One paper per team

Each team may submit no more than one individual method paper.

03

Up to three authors

Each paper may list no more than three authors. The team determines the author list and author order, subject to standard authorship requirements.

04

Final-submission method

The paper should primarily describe the method used in the team’s final challenge submission, together with its experiments, results, and analysis.

Paper format and review

Submission requirements

Format

  • Springer LNCS template
  • Up to 8 pages of main text
  • Up to 2 additional pages of references
  • Full-paper track only; a manuscript may be shorter than 8 pages, but there is no separate short-paper category

Blind light review

  • The initial manuscript must be anonymized
  • Remove author names, affiliations, emails, acknowledgements, and directly identifying information
  • Review focuses on scope, clarity and completeness of the method, appropriate presentation of experiments/results, and basic academic and writing quality
OpenReview visibility: The original article submission, reviews, and author responses will be made publicly available. The camera-ready paper will remain hidden on OpenReview.

Open the MedReason submission venue

OpenReview profile requirement

Create and complete the profiles of all authors as early as possible, preferably using institutional email addresses.

New profiles created without an institutional email will go through a moderation process that can take up to two weeks.

New profiles created with an institutional email will be activated automatically.

Relationship with the joint journal paper

Teams may participate in both

Submitting an individual workshop paper will not affect a team’s opportunity to contribute to the planned joint MedReason Challenge journal paper. This joint-paper opportunity is not limited to the Top 3 teams.

Individual workshop paperYour team’s method

Focus on the team’s own method, experiments, challenge results, and analysis.

Joint journal paperThe challenge as a whole

Focus on challenge design, aggregate evaluation, cross-team comparisons, and broader findings.

Authors should keep the two manuscripts clearly differentiated in scope and contribution.

Presentation arrangements

Oral and poster opportunities

  • The final Top 3 teams will be invited to give oral presentations.
  • Other accepted papers may be presented as posters.
  • In-person attendance, MICCAI registration, and on-site presentation are not mandatory conditions for paper acceptance.
Camera-ready and publication

Requirements after acceptance

Because the organizers must submit the complete proceedings package to the MICCAI Satellite Events Chairs by August 27, 2026, accepted authors should be prepared for a short camera-ready turnaround. The author-facing deadline and upload instructions will be provided with the decision notification.

Accepted authors will be asked to provide:

  • the final compiled paper PDF;
  • complete LaTeX or Word source files;
  • a supplementary PDF, if applicable; and
  • a License to Publish form signed by the corresponding author.

Authors must comply with the Springer Nature Code of Conduct and applicable policies concerning figures and illustrations, ethical approval, and informed consent.

Public release and patent notice

An open-access version of all accepted papers from the MICCAI 2026 Satellite Event MedReason Challenge will be made available on the MICCAI Society website no earlier than one week before the first day of the conference. Authors intending to file patents are responsible for ensuring that all necessary filings are completed prior to this public release.
