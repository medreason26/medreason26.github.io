Overview

Why MedReason?

Medical MLLMs can perform well on in-distribution benchmarks, yet reliability often drops under challenging clinical acquisition settings. MedReason evaluates whether multimodal reasoning remains dependable when image quality and domain conditions shift.

The challenge uses training data and part of the standard evaluation split from Med-CMR, then extends testing with dedicated OOD scenarios including low-dose X-ray and 7T brain MRI.

Task

At-a-glance challenge task

Build one medical multimodal system for two QA formats: multiple-choice and open-ended answers grounded in medical imaging evidence.

Inputs and outputs

Image(s) + question (+ optional context), with answers produced for both MCQ and free-form clinical reasoning prompts.

Evaluation scope

Performance is measured on standard settings and OOD settings with dedicated robustness analysis.
