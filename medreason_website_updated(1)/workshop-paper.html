01
ELIGIBILITY & SCOPE

Who can submit and what to write

All registered teams are eligible.

The opportunity is not restricted to Top 3 teams or teams receiving a particular final ranking.

  • One paper per team.
  • Up to three authors per paper. The team determines the author list and author order, subject to standard authorship requirements.
  • The paper should primarily describe the method used in the team’s final challenge submission, together with experiments, results, and analysis.
  • Participation is optional and does not affect challenge evaluation, final ranking, or award eligibility.
02
FORMAT & REVIEW

Prepare a full, anonymized LNCS manuscript

Paper format

  • Springer LNCS template
  • Up to 8 pages of main text
  • Up to 2 additional pages of references
  • No separate short-paper track; papers may be shorter than 8 pages

Blind light review

  • Remove names, affiliations, email addresses, acknowledgements, and directly identifying information
  • Review focuses on scope, methodological clarity and completeness, presentation of experiments/results, and basic academic and writing quality

IMPORTANT ACCOUNT REQUIREMENT

Use an institutional email whenever possible

New profiles created without an institutional email will go through a moderation process that can take up to two weeks.

New profiles created with an institutional email will be activated automatically.

All authors should create and complete their OpenReview profiles as early as possible.

OpenReview visibility

The original article submission, reviews, and author response will be made publicly available. The camera-ready paper should remain hidden on OpenReview.

04
RELATIONSHIP WITH THE JOINT JOURNAL PAPER

Teams may participate in both publication opportunities

Submitting an individual workshop paper will not affect a team’s opportunity to contribute to the planned joint MedReason Challenge journal paper. The joint-paper opportunity is not limited to the Top 3 teams.

AFTER ACCEPTANCE

Camera-ready and publication requirements

Expect a short turnaround.

The organizers must submit the full proceedings package to the MICCAI Satellite Events Chairs by August 27, 2026. The author-facing camera-ready deadline and instructions will be provided with the decision notification.

  • Final compiled paper PDF
  • Complete LaTeX or Word source files
  • Supplementary PDF, if applicable
  • License to Publish signed by the corresponding author
  • Compliance with Springer Nature policies on conduct, figures and illustrations, ethical approval, and informed consent
Public release and patent notice

An open-access version of all accepted papers from the MICCAI 2026 Satellite Event MedReason Challenge will be made available on the MICCAI Society website no earlier than one week before the first day of the conference. Authors intending to file patents are responsible for ensuring that all necessary filings are completed prior to this public release.
